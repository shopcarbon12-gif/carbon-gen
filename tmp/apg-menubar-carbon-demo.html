APG menubar look — Carbon palette (demo only)

This page shows how your controls could be grouped in a horizontal menubar with dropdown menu panels, following the W3C APG Menu and Menubar pattern. Your live widget today is a single floating panel (more like a dialog + form), not a persistent menubar — this layout is an alternative visual / IA direction, not a drop-in replacement.

Fake storefront content — scroll the page; the dark bar above stays sticky like a desktop app menu. Open Contrast → Invert to imagine the same choice in this pattern.

Interactions here are simplified (click top items to open; click outside to close). A full implementation would add arrow-key roving focus, Escape, and aria-activedescendant or tab stops per APG.

Compared to your current UI: radios/checkboxes become menuitemradio / menuitemcheckbox inside role="menu"; sections become top-level menubar items or nested menus. Branding can stay in the first slot or in the page header — this demo uses text-only for clarity.
